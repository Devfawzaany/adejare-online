Our Story

Designed for people who know who they are.

Adejare Clothing was created for people who believe that what they wear should reflect who they are. Based in Ibadan, Nigeria, the brand brings together premium fabrics, refined tailoring and timeless style to create clothing that inspires confidence.

From Italian Wool and Merino Fabric to Cashmere, American Wool, Australian Wool, 7 Star Fabric and 9 Star Fabric, every material is selected with attention to quality, comfort and appearance.
